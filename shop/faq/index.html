Questions

Before you order

The things people ask us most. If yours is not here, write to us and a person will read it.

Can I buy your games at the big retailers?+

Our games can all be ordered from our own online store, and most are at major retailers too. See our Where to Buy page for the game-by-game list; DisArmory is available for preorder exclusively from our online store.

What should we do if we lose a piece?+

We’re happy to replace lost pieces. Just contact us and we’ll get a replacement to you.

When does DisArmory ship?+

DisArmory is on preorder and ships December 8, 2026. You can order it now and it will be sent as soon as it arrives.

Do you sell to shops and schools?+

Yes. wholesale ordering details are on our main site, and classrooms and libraries can email us for pricing.

Anything about a specific order, whether shipping, delivery or a return, is best answered directly: hourtimegames@gmail.com.
